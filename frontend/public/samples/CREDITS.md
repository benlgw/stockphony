# Instrument samples

The recorded instruments in this folder (guitars, piano, xylophone, electric
bass, organ, cello, saxophone, trumpet) come from **tonejs-instruments** by
Nicholas Brosowsky:

https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments

They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes made for Stockphony: only a subset of each instrument's notes is
included, and the files were re-encoded to 96 kbps mono MP3 to reduce size.
